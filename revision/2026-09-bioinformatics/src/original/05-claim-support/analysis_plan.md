# Additional routes to support the original biological claim

Written after the previous FOUNDIN-PD results and before disease-effect testing in this directory. This is a transparent additional analysis, not preregistration. The aim is to test the original SNCA–autophagy coupling hypothesis using defensible biological grouping and independent evidence. Positive and negative results will both be retained.

## Evidence motivating the new analyses

The FOUNDIN-PD publication reports 96,623 DA neurons and a separate group of 41,267 immature DA neurons expressing serotoninergic markers (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10025424/). The source labels iDA1+iDA2+iDA3 sum to 96,623 and iDA4 contains exactly 41,267 cells. A one-to-one join of both archived annotation tables covers all 416,216 cells. Of the iDA4 cells, 34,234 were previously labelled SerotoninergicNeurons; 37,149 iDA1 cells were previously labelled DopaminergicNeurons. These are stages of annotation, not independent biological observations. We call the regrouped population DA123 and state that its connection to the published broad DA group is inferred from exact counts and corroborating annotations, rather than claiming that a broad-label column exists or that these are fully mature adult neurons.

The independent human postmortem dataset GSE243639 contains 15 sporadic PD and 14 control donors. The original publication assigns Neurons0 to DA neurons (https://doi.org/10.1186/s13024-023-00699-0). Public author annotations contain only 2 PD donors with at least 50 DA nuclei; at a 20-nucleus threshold, 5 PD donors and 8 controls remain. This is a coverage limitation established before effect testing. The author-derived neuronal subclusters omit some nuclei included in the broad neuron annotation. Retain the published subcluster set, document the difference and do not reassign excluded cells to improve sample size.

## Frozen FOUNDIN-PD analysis

- Retain the previous 8 HC versus 28 iPD cohort, the 43-gene detection-eligible panel, equal weighting of repeated cultures within each donor, at least 100 cells per culture/context, and the sex/batch donor model. All previous results remain visible.
- Define DA123 as iDA1–3, immature4 as iDA4, and remaining cells as all cells outside DA123. Compute RNA log-expression partial Pearson correlations and the previously specified rank/QC sensitivity. Include zeros; adjust cellular library size, mitochondrial proportion and constituent source cell labels in pooled groups.
- Test three contexts: DA123 disease effect, the donor-paired DA123-minus-immature4 contrast, and the donor-paired DA123-minus-remaining-cell contrast. Calculate paired differences within the same cultures before averaging within donors. These comparisons address different specificity questions.
- For each of two methods, report every gene effect with HC3 95% intervals, 19,999 donor-level wild-bootstrap draws and the mean-squared-t omnibus. Correct the 258 possible gene comparisons jointly, retaining missing/non-estimable results explicitly.
- Also test the fixed direction profile described in the original manuscript. The seven positive-direction genes are ATG5, BCL2, UVRAG, ATG10, ATG7, MAP1LC3C and MAP1LC3A. The eleven negative-direction genes are OPTN, EPG5, SPG11, RB1CC1, PINK1, SNX4, SNX30, SNX14, CALCOCO2, WIPI2 and ATG16L2. Apply the already-frozen 43-gene eligibility list, leaving 16 genes. A donor's score is the equally weighted mean of signed Fisher z values. No weights or signs are estimated from the new results. In FOUNDIN-PD this remains a post hoc, non-independent test because the manuscript directions were chosen using the original study data.
- Report Holm correction across the 12 new panel/profile tests and, additionally, across these plus the eight previously reported method/context omnibus tests. A newly favourable method must not erase the prior search.
- If a result warrants further attention, assess donor influence and the original genotype grouping as explicitly labelled diagnostics, not replacement primary tests.

## Independent validation and feasibility

- Evaluate two identified independent human-brain resources, GSE243639 and Kamath et al. GSE178265, irrespective of the sign of results. Freeze and document the analysis of each before testing outcomes. Do not keep adding datasets or thresholds until a positive result appears.
- For GSE243639, verify raw nonnegative integer counts, exact cell-ID matching, clinical metadata and the published Neurons0 annotation. Compute complete count-derived QC metrics while streaming the matrix. Distinguish 83,484 broad-QC nuclei, 6,196 broad neurons and the more stringently annotated neuronal subset.
- The primary within-donor co-expression target requires at least 50 DA nuclei per donor and at least five independent donors per arm. GSE243639 fails this gate with only two PD donors. Do not present a cell-level pooled P value as a remedy.
- A fixed, explicitly exploratory 20-nucleus analysis can assess feasibility and directions with 8 HC and 5 PD donors. Adjust cell-level expression for log library size and mitochondrial fraction; model donor scores using disease, age and sex. PMI and RIN enter a specified sensitivity, not outcome-driven variable selection. Report the two methods and direct DA-versus-other-neuron contrast where donor coverage permits, plus the frozen direction score and the full eligible panel. Correct all resulting panel/profile tests together. Individual gene results are exploratory with joint BH correction. The low cell count and tiny donor cohort preclude treating this alone as a persuasive replication.
- Filter external panel genes solely by availability and at least 5% mean donor detection in the eligible DA subset, ignoring diagnosis. Preserve the fixed direction signs and document every unavailable gene; require at least 12 of the original 16 eligible profile genes and a common measured profile within an analysis. Missing expression or undefined correlations are not zero biological effects.
- For Kamath, first obtain author annotations and clinical/donor metadata through public access routes. The downloaded annotation is in a qs archive and the current R environment lacks the qs package. Check available installed readers or alternative public metadata formats. Do not claim access, replication, or completed validation before the annotation and sample provenance are verified.

## Interpretation and deliverables

The original claim has separable requirements: disease-associated coupling, several specified genes or a gene-panel effect, DA specificity, and replication. A significant pooled or signed profile cannot establish every individual edge, regulatory causation, autophagic flux, or HCQ-mediated reversal. Failure to support this specific human-data claim also does not logically refute all of the separate experimental observations in the manuscript.

Deliver complete tables and scripts, a short Chinese decision memo describing how much of the original conclusion can be retained, and evidence-based English reviewer wording. Do not modify the original manuscript or overwrite earlier response versions until new evidence warrants a clearly identified update. Preserve unresolved annotations, small-sample limits and all tested analysis families.
